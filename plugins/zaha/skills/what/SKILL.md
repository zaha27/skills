I didn't understand your last message. Re-explain it:

- Start with one or two sentences on where we are and why it matters.
- Short, plain sentences. Explain each technical term the first time you use it; keep the term itself in English.
- Use one concrete example or everyday analogy if it helps.
- Keep only what I need to follow; drop detail I didn't ask for. Add nothing new unless it's needed to understand.
- End with the one thing you need from me, if any.

Don't repeat the previous message word for word, and don't apologize.
